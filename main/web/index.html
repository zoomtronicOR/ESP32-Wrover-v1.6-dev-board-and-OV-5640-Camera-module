Zones — drag on the image to draw a zone (max 4)

No zones = the whole frame is watched. Line crossing: click Draw line, drag across the passage; the arrow shows the IN direction. Save with Save zones.

Motion detection

camera covered, blinded or turned away

Tamper detection compares the scene's edges with a reference view learned while it is quiet: alarm after 10 s, a new view is accepted after 5 min. Switching the lights off also reads as covered. While motion or tamper detection is enabled the sensor never enters standby (it needs a continuous frame flow), so it runs warmer.

Live

On-device person detection — runs on the ESP32, no server

TensorFlow Lite Micro person model (96×96 grayscale, ~0.7 s per check). While motion is active the model looks at a square around the moving area, so people further away still fill its input. Answers only "person / no person" — no boxes, no other classes.
